# Module 2: Digital Twin Simulation (Gazebo & Unity)

Welcome to Module 2 of the ROS 2 Educational Module! This module focuses on physics-based simulation and digital twin concepts for humanoid robots, designed for AI and software engineering students in Physical AI.

## Overview

In this module, you will learn about:

- **Digital Twin Fundamentals**: Understanding what digital twins are and their role in robotics development
- **Physics Simulation with Gazebo**: Learning about gravity, collisions, dynamics, and simulating humanoid movement
- **High-Fidelity Interaction with Unity**: Exploring visual realism and human-robot interaction concepts
- **Environment Modeling**: Creating realistic environments for humanoid robot simulation
- **Sensor Simulation**: Understanding various sensor types and their applications
- **Unity Integration**: How Unity and Gazebo work together in digital twin implementations

## Learning Objectives

By the end of this module, you will be able to:

1. Explain the concept of digital twins and their importance in robotics
2. Understand the differences between Gazebo and Unity use cases
3. Describe physics simulation concepts including gravity, collisions, and dynamics
4. Understand how to simulate humanoid movement in physics environments
5. Explain Unity's role in creating visually realistic and interactive digital twins
6. Create and manage simulation environments for humanoid robots
7. Understand various sensor types and their simulation in robotics contexts
8. Implement integrated workflows combining Gazebo and Unity

## Prerequisites

- Completion of Module 1 (ROS 2 Fundamentals)
- Basic understanding of robotics concepts
- Familiarity with programming concepts
