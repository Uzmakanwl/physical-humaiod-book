# Introduction to Digital Twins

Digital twins are virtual representations of physical systems that mirror their real-world counterparts in real-time. In robotics, particularly for humanoid robots, a digital twin serves as a virtual laboratory where engineers can test, validate, and optimize robot behaviors before implementing them on the physical robot.
